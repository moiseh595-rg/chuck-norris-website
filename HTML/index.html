Biography (From Wikipedia)

Childhood

Carlos Ray Norris, better known as Chuck Norris, is an American actor born on March 10, 1940, in Ryan, (Oklahoma.)

A martial arts expert, he primarily focused on action films. A native of Ryan, Oklahoma, Carlos Ray Norris He has two younger brothers, one of whom is the Hollywood producer Aaron Norris. His parents are of Native American (Cherokee) and Irish descent. His parents divorced when he was 16, and he moved to California with his mother and brothers. It was there that he finishes his studies at high school and quickly marries his girlfriend, Diane Holechek.

The army

After his marriage, he joined the US Air Force and was sent to Osan Air Force Base in South Korea for his military service. It was there that he acquired the nickname Chuck and began learning Tangsudo. He then returned to the United States. at March Air Force Base in California. He completed his military service in August 1962 without having participated in any combat other than base training.

Combat sports

Upon his return, he worked for Northrop Grumman and opened a karate school frequented by many celebrities, including Steve McQueen. His son Mike was born in 1963, followed by his daughter Dina in 1964 and a second son, Eric, in 1965. Chuck's track record is impressive:

  • He holds a black belt in Tangsudo and Taekwondo.
  • He is the founder of Chun Kuk Do (The Universal Way).
  • He also practiced Karate (7-time world middleweight champion from 1968 to 1974)
  • He also did Judo and Ju-jitsu

He made his film debut in 1968 in The Wrecking Crew. In 1970, his younger brother Weiland died in Vietnam; Chuck dedicated a film to him. later his film Missing in Action. In 1972, he starred alongside Bruce Lee in The Way of the Dragon" and in 1974, McQueen encouraged him to take acting classes at the MGM Studio, under the tutelage of Jonathan Harris. He then went on to a string of successes with film series such as Invasion USA, Missing in Action, and Delta Force. After thirty years of marriage, Norris and Holechek divorced. In 1990, Norris founded the Kick Drugs Out of America association. Renowned since Kick Start. But by the end of the 1980s, Norris was no longer filling theaters as much, and his main producer, Cannon Group, which had produced his films, was bought by MGM after its bankruptcy. He then turned to television. by launching into the series Walker, Texas Ranger in 1993. This series is a success: it has nine seasons and continues to be broadcast on numerous channels around the world.

Over the last twenty years

Over the last twenty years In 1994, at Survivor Series (a WWE pay-per-view), he refereed a Special Guest Referee Casket Match between The Undertaker and Yokozuna. In 1997, he earned his 8th dan (degree) in Taekwondo, which has 10 degrees. This distinction elevates him to the rank of Grand Master. He is one of the first Westerners to whom it was awarded. He remarried in 1998 to former model Gena O'Kelley, who gave birth to twins in 2002: Dakota Alan Norris, a boy, and Danilee Kelly Norris, a girl.
